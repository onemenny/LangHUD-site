ما الجديد

ما الذي تغيّر في كل إصدار من LangHUD، من الأحدث إلى الأقدم. ملاحظات الإصدار نفسها بالإنجليزية. نزّل أحدث إصدار.

Version 0.1.0

2026-11-18

Added

  • A small on-screen reminder shows which keyboard language you are typing in when you start typing in a text field, switch apps, or come back to typing after a pause.
  • An optional spoken cue says the language name, for example "English" or "Hebrew".
  • The menu bar icon shows the current keyboard language.
  • Fix the last typed text: select text that was typed in the wrong keyboard language and press the hot key to convert it to the other language and switch the keyboard to match.
  • Settings for the reminder, the spoken cue, the hot key and starting at login.
  • A Known Limitations window that lists apps where LangHUD cannot see typing.
  • A 14-day free trial from the first launch. After it the spoken cue and text correction need a license; the on-screen reminder stays free. Reinstalling the app does not restart the trial.
  • LangHUD can now check for updates and offer them (never forced). A new version shows in the menu bar icon's menu; "Check for Updates…" is there too.
